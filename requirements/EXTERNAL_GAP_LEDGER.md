## Product acceptance checks

- **B01:** step instances remain distinct from operation definitions; time-scoped lot/split/merge edges carry source/relationship provenance; supported multi-hop lineage traverses only lot-to-lot split/merge edges, not sibling membership; clock uncertainty crossing material-edge effectivity leaves membership/path ambiguous with candidates, and cross-source identity mappings must cover full event-time uncertainty; chronology reports ambiguity; route completeness depends on verified coverage; material-edge completeness accepts only a source-linked, time-scoped entity/type/source declaration and remains caller-declared, not authenticated.
- **B02:** chamber and tool classifications remain separate; actual event bounds and clock uncertainty are honored; `EXPOSED`, `POTENTIALLY_EXPOSED`, `UNKNOWN`, and `NOT_OBSERVED_EXPOSED` are mutually justified only with an approved, effective-dated source relationship for the coverage record and its covered event sources; caller interval labels do not create condition events.
- **B03:** observed variants preserve rework/repeat instances and route versions; only approved expected routes permit conformance classification; incomplete histories are not violations.
- **B05:** every explanation contains support, contradiction, comparators, missingness, confounders, and next verification; statistical association and causal conclusion are separately labeled.
- **MCP:** all six frozen logical operations negotiate and execute over stdio and Streamable HTTP; tests cover server denial, bounds, time/grain/provenance, cancellation, recovery, and matching audit hashes after a recovered HTTP read.
